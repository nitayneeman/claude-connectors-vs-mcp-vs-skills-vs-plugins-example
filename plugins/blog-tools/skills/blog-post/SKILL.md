name: blog-post
description: Drafts a blog post as a page in the Notion blog database. Use when asked to draft, outline or add a blog post.
---

The blog lives in a Notion database called "Blog". Each post is a page with these properties:

- **Title**: the post title, in title case.
- **Slug**: lowercase words separated by hyphens, derived from the title.
- **Excerpt**: one sentence, up to 160 characters, that says what the reader will learn.
- **Published**: a checkbox. Leave it unchecked for drafts.

Create new posts as drafts. Open the body with a short paragraph that states the problem,
then use `##` headings for sections and end with a "Wrapping Up" section that lists the key points.
